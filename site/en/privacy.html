Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026 / Effective: October 18, 2026

This extension collects no personal information.

It has no server of its own and never sends data anywhere other than Bluesky. (Only GIF images are loaded from the GIF provider, the same way Bluesky's own app does it. A video attached to a reply is exchanged with Bluesky's video service) The only things it stores are the settings you choose and the post languages you use for replies. The details follow.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the browser extension "XViewer for Bluesky" (as distributed on GitHub; "the extension") and to this website (xvbluesky.neonsdesign.com).

The extension is an unofficial tool that shows the media of a Bluesky (https://bsky.app/) profile as a thumbnail grid and adds post details, a reply list and action buttons to the image lightbox. It is not made or distributed by Bluesky Social PBC and has no connection with the company. For how Bluesky itself handles personal information, please see Bluesky's privacy policy.

2. Information it does not collect

The extension does not collect any of the following. It does not read, store or send them.

  • Personally identifiable information — name, address, email address, age, identification numbers, etc.
  • Health information
  • Financial and payment information
  • Authentication information — passwords, app passwords, login tokens, credentials, etc.
  • Personal communications — email, DMs, etc.
  • Location — region, IP address, GPS coordinates, etc.
  • Web history — the list of pages you visit and related data
  • User activity — records of clicks, mouse position, scrolling, keystrokes, etc.
  • Website content — storing or sending images, text, audio, video, etc.

The extension loads Bluesky posts and images to show them in the grid and the panel, but they are only handled in your browser's memory to draw them on screen. They are never stored, and never sent anywhere the author or any third party can reach. The same goes for text you type into the reply box in the panel: it is only posted to Bluesky as a reply when you send it, and the extension never stores it or sends it anywhere else.

Images you attach to a reply are handled the same way. Images you pick, paste or drop are resized and turned into JPEG in your browser's memory, and are uploaded to the server that holds your account's data (your PDS) and attached to the reply only when you send it. Images you do not send are discarded when the reply box closes, and are neither stored nor sent.

A video attached to a reply is handled differently. The video you pick, paste or drop is uploaded as the original file, not shrunk, to Bluesky's video service (video.bsky.app) as soon as you attach it, and Bluesky converts it there. This is so that sending can wait until the conversion is finished, and Bluesky's own reply screen works in the same order. The upload happens even if you never send the reply, but removing the video or closing the reply box stops an upload in progress. The video's length, size and aspect ratio, and the alt text and the caption file, are sent to Bluesky together with the reply only when you send it, and the extension never stores them.

When you search for GIFs in the reply box, the search terms, and the URLs of links you write in the reply box, are sent to Bluesky's relay services to get the list of GIFs and the content of link cards. (See "4. Where it connects") These are the same destinations Bluesky's own reply screen uses for the same actions, and the extension never stores them.

3. Information it stores

The extension stores only the settings you choose in the popup, whether the post details panel is collapsed, and the post languages you pick in the reply box. The settings and the collapsed state are kept in the browser's sync storage (chrome.storage.sync), which lives in your browser and, if you have browser sync turned on, in that browser account (your Google account for Chrome, Microsoft account for Edge, Mozilla account for Firefox). Neither the author nor any third party can read it.

The following 9 values are kept in sync storage. Each is either true/false or one of a fixed set of choices.

  • Whether to show the media grid
  • Whether to remove the NSFW blur
  • Whether to show the post details panel
  • Whether to show the reply and like buttons in the panel
  • Whether to show the reply list in the panel
  • Whether to keep the lightbox open when the image is clicked
  • Whether to use debug mode
  • The popup's theme (follow the OS / dark / light)
  • Whether the post details panel is collapsed

Separately, the post languages you pick in the reply box (up to 3 language codes, such as en) are kept as a single value in this browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local), so they become the default for your next reply. This value is not synced and never leaves this browser.

IDs of posts or accounts you view, your browsing history, records of your likes or reposts, the replies you write, and the images you attach are never stored.

When debug mode is on, the extension writes its internal state (the current tab, whether the grid is running, how many items were fetched, etc.) to the browser console and to an attribute on the html element of the open page. This exists only inside that page and is neither stored nor sent. It disappears when you close the page.

Deleting stored information

Pressing "Reset settings" in the popup restores the stored values to their defaults. The post languages are replaced whenever you pick languages again in the reply box. Removing the extension from your browser deletes both storage areas entirely.

4. Where it connects

The extension has no servers. It never connects to any server run by the author. The only connections the extension makes on its own are to Bluesky's public API, and to the same relay services and GIF provider that Bluesky's own app uses for GIFs and link cards in replies, plus Bluesky's video service for videos in replies.

  • https://public.api.bsky.app/ — fetching the profile you are viewing, its list of posts, the post you open in the lightbox, the replies to that post (when Bluesky's own client cannot be borrowed), your own avatar for the reply box, and the accounts you mention in a reply or whose post URL you paste to quote it (the account identifier for each handle). This is a public API that needs no sign-in
  • https://gifs.bsky.app/ — listing and searching GIFs when you open the GIF search in the reply box. This is Bluesky's relay for its GIF search (KLIPY); it receives only the search terms, the region of your browser language (for example jp) and the position of the next page
  • https://static.klipy.com/ / https://k.gifs.bsky.app/ — loading GIF images and videos in the GIF search, the reply box preview and the reply list, and fetching the thumbnail when you send a reply with a GIF. These only load images; nothing about you is sent
  • https://t.gifs.bsky.app/ — loading the videos of Tenor GIFs in the reply list
  • https://video.bsky.app/ — uploading a video you attach to a reply, and playing videos in the reply list. This is the video service run by Bluesky. For uploading it receives the video file and a short-lived authorization that proves your account (created by Bluesky's own client in the page; the extension never stores it and never hands it to any other part of the extension). For playback it only loads the video's playlist, the video segments and the thumbnail, and nothing about you is sent
  • https://cardyb.bsky.app/ — fetching the link card (title, description and image) for a link you write in the reply box. This is a service run by Bluesky; it receives only the URL of that link. Card images are also loaded from here

The GIF and link card connections happen only when you open the GIF search, when you write a link in the reply box (when you paste a link or type a space after it), and when a reply in the list has a GIF or a card. All of these servers allow extensions to load from them, so no extra permission is requested.

The video connections happen only when you attach a video in the reply box (uploading) and when you click a video card in the reply list (playback). Only the thumbnail is loaded whenever a reply in the list has a video, to draw the card. The video service also allows extensions to load from it, so no extra permission is requested.

Grid thumbnails show the image URLs that Bluesky attaches to posts (served by Bluesky's image servers) as they are. This is the same loading that happens when you browse Bluesky normally.

When it loads the reply list, and when you like, repost, bookmark, send a reply (including uploading the images and caption file attached to it) or delete your own reply from the panel, the extension borrows the signed-in client that Bluesky keeps in the page and sends the request to the server that holds your account's data (your PDS). The thumbnails of link cards and GIFs and the caption file are also uploaded through it when you send the reply. For a video upload, the short-lived authorization this client creates is used on the spot only to send to the video service and your PDS, and is never read out, stored or handed on, and the post you quote is fetched through it too. Bluesky decides where to send it and handles the tokens; the extension does not read the tokens. It is the same request Bluesky makes when you press its own buttons. The reply list is read from the public API above only when that client cannot be borrowed. The "Quote" item of the repost button only opens Bluesky's own composer; the post made there is made by Bluesky when you send it.

The emoji picker in the reply box is a page bundled with the extension (Emoji Mart) opened inside the panel, and the list of emoji is bundled too. Nothing is loaded from outside, and the emoji you pick only goes into the text box.

Videos in the reply list are played with a player component bundled with the extension (hls.js). It is loaded only when you click a video card, and the only thing loaded from outside is the video itself.

No analytics, advertising or crash reporting of any kind is built in.

5. Permissions and why they are needed

The extension asks for only these two permissions. Its scripts run only on bsky.app pages, and it asks for no access to other sites.

storage

Used to store the settings described above. Settings are changed in the toolbar popup and read by the script running on Bluesky pages, so a way to share values between the two is needed. It is also used to remember the post languages you pick in the reply box.

https://public.api.bsky.app/*

Used to fetch a profile's posts, the details and replies of an opened post, and the account information needed for the reply box and mentions from Bluesky's public API. It is limited to this one domain.

6. How sign-in information is handled

The extension does not read your Bluesky login tokens or password. Bluesky keeps its sign-in information in your browser's storage, but the extension does not touch it.

To send likes, reposts, bookmarks and replies, and to load the reply list, it borrows the signed-in client that Bluesky keeps in the page. The only thing it reads is your own account identifier (DID). It uses it only as the destination for recording your own likes, reposts and replies in your own account, and to look up your own avatar for the reply box from Bluesky's public API. It neither stores it nor sends it anywhere other than Bluesky.

What can be done through the borrowed client is limited to adding and removing your own likes, reposts and bookmarks, fetching post details and replies, creating and deleting replies in your own account, and uploading a video to attach to a reply. Only replies that point to the post they answer can be created. The only things that can be attached are images (up to 10, with alt text), a video (one, with alt text and a caption file), a GIF or a link card (both are embeds of an http or https link), a quote of a post, and content warnings for adult content or graphic media (labels the author sets on their own post); other embeds such as quotes of feeds or lists cannot be attached. Only JPEG, PNG, WebP and GIF files of up to 2MB can be uploaded as images, only .vtt files of up to 20KB as captions, and only mp4, webm, mov and mpeg files of up to 300MB as videos. A post can be deleted only after the extension has checked with Bluesky that it is a reply in your own account. Creating or deleting posts that are not replies, following, blocking and sending DMs are not possible through it.

Write actions run only when you press a button in the panel (for a reply, "Send" or Ctrl+Enter / Cmd+Enter). Nothing is sent automatically. The only thing done on its own is loading the reply list when you open an image, while "Reply list" is turned on in the settings.

7. Sharing with third parties

Since nothing is collected, there is nothing to share with third parties. The extension never sells, transfers or shares user data. It does not use data for purposes unrelated to its single purpose (making Bluesky media and the image lightbox easier to use), and never uses it to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

8. About this website

This website is a set of static pages published on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). It uses no analytics, advertising, external fonts or external scripts. It sets no cookies. Only when you switch the color theme does it remember your choice in your browser's storage (localStorage).

GitHub, Inc., the hosting provider, may record access logs (such as IP addresses) as part of providing its service. These are not managed by the author. For details, please see GitHub's privacy statement.

The screenshots on this website are real screens of the public profile of Bluesky's official account (@bsky.app), shown in a browser with this extension installed. The posts, images and counts are unedited. Only the avatar of the account used to take them and the "followed by" line were hidden.

9. Use by children

The extension collects no personal information from any user, regardless of age. Removing the NSFW blur is off by default and affects only the grid tiles the extension shows. It never changes Bluesky's own content settings or the warnings Bluesky shows.
